An audit of an agent that reads manufacturing quote requests, flags the specs a customer left out, and holds the price until they arrive. This page shows how often that flag is right.

    Limits

    • The emails are synthetic: generated from templates, then paraphrased by a small local model. Real inboxes are messier.
    • There are 30 requests, so every rate has a wide interval. A zero is a result, not a guarantee.
    • One labeller wrote the labels, before the model ran. The checker and the labels follow the same written policy, so some agreement is by construction.
    • Prices come from invented rules and 80 invented past jobs. They say nothing about any real market.
